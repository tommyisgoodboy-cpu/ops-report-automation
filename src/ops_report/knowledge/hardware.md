# Hardware troubleshooting

## Laptop will not power on
Check the charger and power outlet, then hold the power button for 15 seconds to drain residual power. Remove the laptop from the docking station and try again. If there is still no sign of life, raise a hardware repair and prepare a loan laptop.

## Monitor flickers or has no signal
Reseat the display cable at both ends and try another cable or port. Update the graphics driver. If the flicker follows the monitor to another PC, replace the monitor.
